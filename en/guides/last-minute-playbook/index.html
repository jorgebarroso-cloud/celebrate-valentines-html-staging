Last-minute Valentine’s playbook

Fast steps to book before peak week sells out.

Step 1 — City & date

Open last-minute in your city; keep peak week (Feb 7–15) or switch to All dates if you need flexibility.

Step 2 — Price

Use Under 25 / 25–50 chips if budgeting; Candlelight often has clear tiers.

Step 3 — Book

Open the plan and checkout on Fever immediately. Session inventory moves fast.

Tips

If dinners are sold out, try workshops or concerts. Experience gifts still work same-day.
