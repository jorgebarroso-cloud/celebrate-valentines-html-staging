Who we are

Celebrate Valentine's is operated by Fever Up Entertainment. We help couples find romantic dinners, Candlelight concerts, workshops, and gift experiences in cities worldwide — then book securely on Fever.

How we work

We sync Fever Valentine and Candlelight inventory, score plans for romantic fit, and surface Top picks, categories, and intent hubs (restaurants, gifts, ideas, last-minute). You always complete checkout on Fever.

Why trust this guide

We publish editorial standards, selection methodology, and clear legal/cookie policies. We do not invent star ratings in structured data. Couple stories on the site are labeled as illustrative editorial content.
